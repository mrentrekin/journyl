Journyl Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Journyl is a journaling app. It was built so that what you write stays yours. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

Journyl does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no server that stores your journal, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you write and log stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your journal entries, moods, feelings, photos, voice notes, places, lesson progress, sleep log and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it, so use Export journyl in Settings to keep a copy.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Journyl reads sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate, steps, exercise minutes, active energy, State of Mind and mindful minutes. It uses them on your iPhone to show your sleep, recovery, stress and strain and your week at a glance. Journyl never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never sent off your device, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Location, camera, photos and microphone

Journyl asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them: your location when you tap the pin to add a place to an entry, the camera or photo library when you add a photo or save a quote image, and the microphone when you record a voice note. What you capture is stored with that entry on your device. Place names are looked up through Apple's own location service.

Articles and podcasts

The Grow tab loads public article and podcast feeds from their publishers. These requests do not include anything about you, though the publishers' servers see a normal web request, as with any website. Articles open in Safari inside the app, where each publisher's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Journyl is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Journyl shares education, not treatment, and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Journyl ever begins collecting data, the policy and the App Store listing will say so before it does.
