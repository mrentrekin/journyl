Journyl Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email mrentrekin@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account or cloud copy, so use Export journyl in Settings regularly if you want a backup.

How do I connect Apple Health?

On Today, swipe the top card to the Body or Mind & body card and tap Connect Apple Health. You can change access later in the Health app.

How do I mark a lesson as learned?

Read through to the last card of the lesson, write your answer to the prompt, and tap Save and finish. Your answer is saved to your journal.

Switching between clinical and everyday topics

In Learn, tap All a second time to switch the topic pills between therapy names (like Dialectical behavior therapy) and everyday topics (like Thoughts).

Need help now?

Journyl is not a crisis service. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
